# Knowledge Base identity

The blue-jay mascot represents a Knowledge Base that agents and people can keep
building together. Its folded wing suggests pages; cobalt and ice blue connect
it to the website’s Midnight blue palette. Use the name **Knowledge Base** beside
the mark when context is missing. The CLI executable remains `kb`.

[logo.png](logo.png) is the transparent master shared by the README and website.
The Astro build produces smaller local header and favicon assets from this
master; do not serve the full source image as a favicon. Keep its aspect ratio,
alpha channel and enough breathing room. Avoid adding a background tile or
stretching the bird to fill a non-square frame. Check both light and dark
backgrounds and small sizes when changing the mark.

The original monogram and unused SVG wordmark have been retired. This is a
raster identity asset. Repository licensing is specified in
[LICENSE](../LICENSE).

## Generation record

Created using the built-in image generation tool, then refined with the first
output as its reference. No external API credentials or paid logo service are
required to reproduce this workflow. The final prompt was:

> Use case: logo-brand. Refine this blue-jay Knowledge Base mascot into its final
> small-size brand mark. Keep the friendly confident bird identity, cobalt and
> ice-blue palette, expressive eye and folded-page wing motif. Change only graphic
> simplification and framing: make it a compact circular bird bust (head and single
> folded wing, no feet and no long tail), with a very simple bold silhouette that
> reads at 24–32 px. Remove tiny feather details and all shading/gradients/bevels:
> 4 flat solid colors only, deep navy #071724, cobalt #3662EA, ice blue #A8C7FF,
> ivory #F3F5EE. Use the ice-blue silhouette/edges where needed so it reads on dark
> navy as well as white. Professional independent open-source identity, warm and
> crisp, avoid a sports-team angry expression. One centered mark, even square
> margins, transparent alpha background. No text/letters/initials, tile, circle
> background, shadows, scene, watermark or multiple variants.

The generated result retains subtle tonal variation. It is the selected image,
not a claim that every prompt constraint was followed exactly. Future vector
or monochrome variants should be reviewed as additional deliverables.
